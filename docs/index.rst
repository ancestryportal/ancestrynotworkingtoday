Whether the website won't load, the mobile app is crashing, or you're seeing strange error messages, this guide provides immediate troubleshooting steps to get you back on track. From checking service status to resolving browser and app issues, these solutions address the most common reasons Ancestry may be unavailable right now.

Check if Ancestry Is Down Today
===============================

Before troubleshooting your own device, determine whether Ancestry is experiencing a widespread outage today. Service disruptions can affect the website, mobile app, or specific features like DNA-related functionality [citation:1][citation:6][citation:10].

Check the Ancestry site status page to see if there are any reported service interruptions [citation:5]. If there is a known outage, you may need to wait for Ancestry to resolve the problem. Status monitoring services have reported recent incidents where the website and app were unavailable, with issues lasting from 24 minutes to over five hours in some cases [citation:1][citation:6].

If the status page shows normal operation but Ancestry is still not working for you today, the issue is likely on your end. Continue with the following troubleshooting steps.

Browser-Specific Problems
=========================

If Ancestry is not working in your web browser today, the issue is often browser-related. Ancestry recommends using supported browsers for the best experience: Google Chrome (desktop and mobile), Mozilla Firefox (desktop), and Apple Safari (mobile) [citation:4].

**Update Your Browser**

Ensure your browser is up to date. Outdated browsers may have reduced functionality or may not work at all with Ancestry [citation:4]. Go to your browser's settings to find your current version and the option to update.

**Clear Cache and Cookies**

Clearing your browser's cache and cookies resolves many display and loading issues [citation:8][citation:12]. If your cache and cookies have not been cleared recently, your browser may try to load a previous version of a page rather than the current one [citation:3]. After clearing, close the browser completely and open it again.

**Disable Browser Extensions**

Browser extensions, including ad blockers and privacy tools, can interfere with Ancestry [citation:9][citation:13]. Try temporarily disabling all extensions to see if Ancestry works. If it does, re-enable extensions one by one to identify the culprit.

**Adjust Privacy and Security Settings**

If your browser's privacy and security settings are too high or strict, they can prevent Ancestry pages from displaying correctly. For Firefox users, Enhanced Tracking Protection can sometimes block content—click the shield icon in the address bar to check and disable protection if needed [citation:13].

**Try a Different Browser**

If Ancestry works in one browser but not another, the issue is browser-specific. For example, some users have reported that Ancestry works in Chrome but freezes in Firefox, particularly when manipulating family trees [citation:13].

Mobile App Not Working Today
============================

If the Ancestry mobile app is not working today, the problem may be app-specific. The app may crash, freeze, fail to open, or not display trees correctly.

**Close and Restart the App**

Start by closing the app completely and reopening it. This simple step often resolves temporary issues [citation:3].

**Update the App**

Ensure you have the latest version of the Ancestry app. Ancestry periodically releases new versions to provide new features and fix issues [citation:3]. If you cannot update the app, check your device for system updates.

**Restart Your Device**

Restarting your phone or tablet can clear temporary system issues that may be causing the app to malfunction [citation:3].

**Uninstall and Reinstall the App**

If the app continues to have problems, uninstall and reinstall the app. Uninstalling the app removes it from your device but does not erase your data, which is stored on the Ancestry website. After reinstalling, sign in to your account again [citation:3].

**System Requirements**

The Ancestry app requires iOS version 15.0 or higher and Android 7.0 or higher [citation:3]. If your device is older and not supported, consider switching to a supported device or using the Ancestry website.

The “Pretty-Print” Issue
========================

If Ancestry displays “pretty-print { }” instead of loading properly today, your IP address may have been blocked [citation:2]. This can occur even without a VPN and may persist across multiple browsers and devices [citation:2].

**Use a VPN**

Access Ancestry via a VPN to change your public IP address. The free VPN built into the Firefox browser can be a simple solution [citation:2].

**Try a Different Network**

Connect from a different network, such as mobile data or a different Wi-Fi network. Changing your public IP address often resolves the issue [citation:2][citation:7].

Security Software and VPNs
==========================

Security software, including antivirus programs, firewalls, internet security suites, and VPNs, may block content Ancestry needs to work properly [citation:5]. If parts of Ancestry are not loading correctly, your membership is not being recognized, or you are seeing unexpected errors today, security software may be the cause.

**Temporarily Disable Security Software**

To determine whether security software is causing the issue, temporarily turn it off, test Ancestry, and then turn it back on [citation:5]. On a computer, access your security software from the menu bar (Mac) or system tray (Windows). On a mobile device, temporarily turn off any VPN or security app.

**Add Ancestry to the Allowed List**

If Ancestry works while your security software is disabled, add Ancestry to the allowed or trusted list in your security software. You may need to allow the following domains:

- *.ancestry.com
- *.ancestrycdn.com
- *.search.ancestry.com
- *.purchase.ancestry.com
- *.pointofsale.ancestry.com

These addresses should also be added to any ad blocker's allowed list [citation:5][citation:9].

Internet Connection Problems
============================

If you receive messages saying “You appear to be offline” or “Can't connect to the internet,” your device may be in airplane mode or not connected to Wi-Fi or a cellular network [citation:3].

- Check that airplane mode is turned off [citation:3].
- Connect to the internet via Wi-Fi, hotspot, or cellular network [citation:3].
- If you can't connect to a Wi-Fi network, consult your device manufacturer's support resources [citation:3].

Login Problems Today
====================

If you cannot sign in to Ancestry today, try the following steps:

- Verify your email address and password are correct.
- Use the "Forgot password" link to reset your password if needed.
- Try signing in through a recommended browser. If you can sign in through the browser but not the app, uninstall and reinstall the app [citation:3].
- If you cannot sign in using either the app or a browser, reset your password.

Access Denied Error 21
======================

If you encounter “Access Denied Error 21,” your request has been blocked by security rules [citation:7]. This is typically an IP-related issue similar to the “pretty-print” problem. Try the same solutions: use a VPN or connect from a different network.

Conclusion
==========

When Ancestry is not working today, the cause is often related to IP blocking, browser issues, security software conflicts, or mobile app problems. By systematically checking for service outages, updating your browser or app, clearing cache and cookies, adjusting security settings, and using a VPN if needed, you can resolve most issues quickly. If none of these steps resolve the problem, contact Ancestry support for further assistance.
